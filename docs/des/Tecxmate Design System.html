01 · Principles

Clarity, craft, and quiet confidence.

Four values guide every surface we ship — from the marketing site to the onboarding form to any client-facing tool we build. Hold these before reaching for type, color, or components.

01

Apple-clean by default

System fonts, generous whitespace, neutral grays, subtle shadows. The UI gets out of the way so the work speaks.

02

Purple is a tool, not décor

Reserve #8c52ff for brand marks, primary actions, and section statements — never for decoration.

03

Square, not rounded

Cards, thumbnails, and tiles use rounded-none. Save radius for buttons, pills, and form fields.

04

Glow on hover

Interactive cards light up with a colored border and ambient shadow matching their category — not a blunt elevation.

Added from product work v1.1
05

Fewer moving parts

When something opens, one element changes and everything else stays still. Count the elements and the things that move. Remove one of each if you can.

06

The same place in every state

A control keeps its position when a card opens, a filter is set or the page is dragged. A screen looks the same at rest and in motion.

07

Highlight only what was chosen

An overview singles nothing out. An area, a row or a figure is emphasised only after the reader selects it.

08

An honest state for everything

Loading, failed, withheld and zero each look different. A failure never shows as a number.

This is the convention in Taiwan, and we keep it in every language. Each figure also carries ▲ or ▼, so colour is never the only sign.

Bars, dots and rankings use the brand purple. Only a change over time takes the direction colours.

Gold means part of a paid plan v1.1

Gold marks a locked row or a paid feature. It has no other use.

Small white text sits on the darker purple v1.1

Both themes are designed and both are checked v1.1

Rules for product text v1.1

Labels never wrap v1.1

A button, tab, chip, pill or menu label stays on one line. Shorten the words first. If they still do not fit, the label ends in an ellipsis.

Names may take two lines v1.1

An industry, a company or a place can run to a second line in a list, then an ellipsis. The numbers beside it stay on one line. The detail screen shows the full name.

Chinese uses the device face v1.1

PingFang TC or Noto Sans TC for interface text, and Noto Serif TC beside Instrument Serif. The interface needs no web font.

Every name is stored as its owner wrote it v1.1

Chinese, Vietnamese, Arabic, accents and emoji survive sign-up, storage and display. Each new form is tested with them.

Exports embed their fonts v1.1

A tool that makes a PDF or a video has no reader device to fall back on. It embeds a Traditional Chinese font.

Text fields are 16px or larger v1.1

A field keeps the page still when it takes focus. The page never zooms, scrolls or shifts under the keyboard.

09 · Motion v1.1

Short, smooth, and quiet.

Three durations and two curves cover every product. Motion explains where something came from. It never performs.

TokenValueUse
--dur-tap120msA press.
--dur-ui200msA colour, a chevron, a pill, a popover.
--dur-panel300msA panel, a sheet, a dialog, a page.
--ease-standardcubic-bezier(.4, 0, .2, 1)The default, and every exit.
--ease-decelcubic-bezier(0, 0, .2, 1)Entrances.

One set of tokens v1.1

No transition names a raw duration or curve. A build check fails if one does.

Only transform and opacity move v1.1

Width, height, position and clip paths are never animated. A panel that grows is laid out at its final size and revealed through a window that slides.

Nothing is measured while a finger moves v1.1

Sizes are read once when the drag starts. Each frame sets one transform. A map is never redrawn per frame.

A drag follows the finger v1.1

The release decides by distance or by speed. A long drag or a quick flick closes a card. A short one springs back.

Short and smooth, never a snap v1.1

Every change of state has a transition from the token set. None of them is a show.

Reduced motion means no transition v1.1

When the reader turns animation off, the new state applies in the same step as the tap. A shortened animation is still an animation.

10 · Product UI v1.1

Phones, maps, and cards.

Rules for products people hold in one hand. They came from building 臺灣地圖集 Taiwan Atlas, and they apply to any app with a map, a list or a sliding card.

Card heights and search on a phone v1.1
499,729
HalfThe card opens at half height. The map and its controls stay in use above it.
FullDragged up, the card reaches the top of the screen. No strip of map is left above it.
咖啡
SearchSearch takes the whole screen. The field sits at the top and the results are a list. Choosing one returns to the map.

A card has two heights v1.1

Half shows the map above it. Full reaches the top of the screen. No height in between leaves a thin strip of map.

Search is a full screen v1.1

Typing opens a full-screen list of results with the field at the top. The map returns when the reader picks a result or goes back.

A handle that shows is a handle that works v1.1

Every card that slides up carries the same handle. A swipe down on it closes the card. Scrolling the content still scrolls.

One card answers one question v1.1

A card has at most two levels: a summary with tabs, then one detail screen with one way back to the tab the reader left.

A selector at the top sets the scope below v1.1

A period or an area selector changes the headline, the chart, the lists and the detail together.

The title and the content name the same place v1.1

A sheet titled Taipei City never shows another county under it.

The count is the legend v1.1

The control that explains a map shows the number of things on screen. It grows in place into the full legend. There is no second panel.

A toolbar is a column and keeps its shape v1.1

On a phone the tools stack in one column. The column does not change when a card or the legend opens.

The source credit is always on screen v1.1

Open-data licences require it in every state of the page. Notices sit on the left. The one offer sits on the right and is slightly heavier.

One blocking dialog at a time v1.1

A second dialog waits for the first to close.

A button beside each gesture v1.1

Anything a pinch or a swipe can do, a visible button can also do.

One map per page v1.1

Each page has one map with its own layers. Layers do not pile up across pages, and the map does not rotate.

Results and sources go out, the method stays in v1.1

A screen, a document, an API reply, an AI connector and a post say what a number is and where it comes from. None of them explains how it was produced.

Every number carries its source and its date v1.1

The source and the date sit beside the figure or directly under the list.

Zero is a real answer v1.1

Loading and failure show an ellipsis. Zero is kept for real zeros.

Small counts are hidden v1.1

A count of 1 to 4 shows as <5. So does any figure that would let a reader work one out by subtraction.

People are counts, never rows v1.1

A sole proprietor or a partnership is a person. Their name, id and address stay out of anything paid, exported or sent to a client.

An estimate is labelled an estimate v1.1

The product never presents a figure it does not hold as a fact.

Arrows share a column and figures share another v1.1

Figures are right-aligned with tabular numerals. A unit that repeats on every row is said once, under the list.

A change needs two positive amounts v1.1

Without them the row shows no percentage.

A period selector works like a stock viewer v1.1

Short labels such as 2M, 1Y, 3Y and 5Y. One plain sentence says what is compared with what.

Paid content is locked on the server v1.1

A free reader sees the lock, one sample row and where to get the rest. The paid rows are never sent to their browser.

Plain, confident, bilingual.

Speak as an equal v1.1

Lead with our own name and what we know. The copy does not address the customer's size or ask for their business.

Plain words and full sentences v1.1

A client should not need our vocabulary. Words such as layer, coordinate, API and CSV stay out of client text.

Say what it is v1.1

A sentence states what a thing is. It does not define the thing by what it is not.

One name for one thing v1.1

If two things in a product carry the same words, one of them is renamed.

A label states what will happen v1.1

An error says what went wrong and what to do next.

The local language comes first v1.1

Chinese first with English beside it, through one mechanism. English mode shows no Chinese except a proper name with no English form.

Shorten the longer language v1.1

If four labels fit in Chinese and not in English, the English is shortened. The page the label opens keeps the full title.

Official names and Traditional characters v1.1

Taiwan products write 臺北市, as the government does.

13 · Practice v1.1

How we change a screen.

The working order that keeps a public product stable while it changes every day.

A mock-up comes first v1.1

Anything visual is mocked up on real screenshots and real figures. The build starts after the owner approves it.

Change only what was asked v1.1

Anything else that looks wrong is noted and raised. It is not changed on the side.

Check it where the reader will see it v1.1

A phone at 390 × 844, a slowed processor, dark and light, and the real address the reader opens. A preview behind a sign-in wall must load its data like the live site.

A check fails before the fix and passes after it v1.1

A check that only ever passed proves nothing.

A timing check waits for the settled state v1.1

It polls for the final state within a limit. It never counts frames.

Stage first, then release v1.1

The public site only receives a build that passed every check and that the owner tried on the preview.
