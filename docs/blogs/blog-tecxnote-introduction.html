Career fairs are exciting, but they are also chaotic. Students wander between booths, recruiters juggle interview requests, and event organizers scramble to keep schedules aligned. TECXWORK is the web platform built to make V-GEN TRIDENT 2026 work smoothly for everyone: students, recruiters, and event staff.

What is TECXWORK?

TECXWORK is a modern job fair platform created by TECXMATE for the Vietnamese Student Association in Taiwan (VSATW). It powers the V-GEN TRIDENT career fair experience with recruiter profiles, job postings, interview slot booking, and applicant discovery.

Instead of managing paper sign-up sheets, email threads, and last-minute schedule changes, TECXWORK centralizes the event into one platform that students and recruiters can use before, during, and after the fair.

The Problem We're Solving

Traditional career fairs are often built on friction:

  1. Students arrive unsure which companies will hire.
  2. Recruiters collect resumes in a paper stack.
  3. Interview requests are exchanged manually or via chat.
  4. Organizers spend the day updating spreadsheets and chasing no-shows.

For the V-GEN TRIDENT event, TECXWORK eliminates that friction with a digital workflow designed around the real experience of a one-day job fair.

How TECXWORK Works

The platform supports three core groups:

  • Students can register, build a profile, browse participating companies, explore open positions, and request interview slots.
  • Recruiters can sign up, publish company information, post approved job openings, and manage interview bookings with candidates.
  • Admins configure the event, generate interview slots for V-GEN TRIDENT 2026, manage homepage content, and oversee booking direction and moderation.

Every step is built to support the event flow for V-GEN TRIDENT:

  • Homepage & company directory show who is attending and what roles they are hiring for.
  • Job board lets students search open positions by company, location, and role.
  • Interview booking creates 15-minute slots on event day between 10:00 AM and 5:30 PM.
  • Profile discovery allows recruiters to view student CV links only when consent has been granted.

Built for V-GEN TRIDENT

TECXWORK is not a generic hiring portal. It is tuned for the realities of a university career fair hosted at NTUT (Taipei Tech) with Vietnamese student communities in mind.

  • Event-first design — The site is configured around the V-GEN TRIDENT timeline, event name, and venue.
  • Bilingual and local-aware — The system supports students who need Vietnamese, Traditional Chinese, and English language experiences.
  • Privacy-safe booking — Student CV links are shared only with recruiters they book with, and booking data is purged after the event.
  • Career fair operations — Admins can adjust booking direction, manage moderation, and publish homepage images for the event.

Smart Workflows for Students and Recruiters

TECXWORK supports the kinds of workflows that make a career fair feel organized instead of chaotic.

  • Students can browse participating companies, review open roles, and choose the companies they want to meet with.
  • Recruiters can post approved job openings, review applicant profiles, and accept or decline interview requests.
  • Admins can lock the event mode, configure slot duration, and review all active bookings from a central dashboard.

Why This Matters

For students, TECXWORK turns uncertainty into opportunity. For recruiters, it turns noisy booth traffic into scheduled conversations with qualified candidates. For event organizers, it turns a one-day fair into a repeatable digital process that can scale to future events.
