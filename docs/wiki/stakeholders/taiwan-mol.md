## Role
The government regulatory body in Taiwan responsible for labor laws, work permits, and employment service licensing.

## Relevance to Project
- **Employment Service Act**: Regulates platforms that connect employers and job seekers.
- **Licensing**: For-profit platforms must obtain a "Private Employment Service Agency" license.
- **Work Permits**: Regulates the legality of student work and employer hiring practices.
- **Compliance**: The Platform must ensure it does not violate regulations regarding "matching" vs. "scheduling operations."
