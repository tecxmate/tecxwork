## Role

Client / sponsoring stakeholder. A Taiwanese manpower and employment-services company operating the Vietnam → Taiwan labour corridor.

**Confirmed 2026-07-27 by [niko]: Yang Luck is the licensed operator of the platform** — i.e. the licensee under Taiwan's Employment Service Act (私立就業服務機構), not merely a prospective customer of a third-party tool. This is the load-bearing fact for product strategy; see [2026-07-27-yang-luck-licensee-positioning](../decisions/2026-07-27-yang-luck-licensee-positioning.md).

## Why the licensee status matters

Because Yang Luck holds the licence, the platform is **not** obliged to route around the ESA. The prior "position as event operations software to avoid licensing" posture recorded in [taiwan-compliance](../topics/taiwan-compliance.md) was written for a scenario where Tecxmate shipped an unlicensed independent product. It does not bind work done under Yang Luck's licence, and regulated broker activity (for-profit matching, employer service fees, worker placement lifecycle) is available as product surface.

## Areas of responsibility

- Employer-side client relationships — 25 client companies seeded into the demo as real recruiters.
- Regulated placement activity: worker recruitment, permit/visa processing, employer transfer (轉換雇主), contract renewal.
- Exposure to MOL's periodic private-employment-agency evaluation (私立就業服務機構評鑑) — a scored, publicly-published audit.
