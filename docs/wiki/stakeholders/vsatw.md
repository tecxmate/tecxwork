## Role
The primary event partner and sponsor for the V-GEN series of career fairs. VSA Taiwan represents the Vietnamese student community in Taiwan and acts as the bridge between students and recruiters.

## Key Contacts
- **Lê Thị Phương Linh**: Representative (MOU signatory).

## Responsibilities
- Event organization and on-the-ground operations for V-GEN TRIDENT.
- Marketing and outreach to Vietnamese students in Taiwan.
- Onboarding recruiters and managing employer relations.
- Providing operational feedback for platform improvements.

## Agreement (MOU)
- **Cost**: VSA Taiwan pays a maximum of 5,000 NTD/year for operation costs.
- **Development**: System development provided by Tecxmate for free in exchange for branding and data management rights.
- **Compliance**: Ensures students are not charged for using the platform.
