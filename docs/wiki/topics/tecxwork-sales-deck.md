## Summary
A customer/sales pitch deck for TECXWORK aimed at **event organizers** (university career offices, student associations like VSA Taiwan, nonprofits, talent agencies) — to win them as the platform for their next career fair. Distinct from the [[saas-strategy]] investor angle and the prior Tecxmate agency case-study framing (see log 2026-06-18). Editable content script lives at `docs/decks/tecxwork-sales-deck.md`.
