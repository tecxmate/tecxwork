## Summary
Tecxmate should quote tecxwork-style systems as event-critical business platforms, not as raw development hours. Project pricing should include product judgment, deployment, operational responsibility, fixes, support, and launch risk.

## Quote Bands
- **Friendly/internal build:** 300k-500k NTD for limited scope, one event, light support, and rough-edge tolerance.
- **Fair commercial project:** 600k-900k NTD for the current tecxwork-style scope: student/recruiter/admin portals, booking logic, email flows, localization, job posting, profile/CV, moderation, dashboards, deployment, and incident fixes.
- **Agency/enterprise quote:** 1.0m-1.6m NTD+ when including formal QA, documentation, warranty, stakeholder meetings, design polish, security review, data export, post-event support, and change requests.

## Comparison
Niko noted imood was quoted around 900k NTD for a 9-month development-hour framing, while estimating real development hours were likely much shorter. The reference point supports treating 900k NTD as a project-retainer / responsibility price rather than a literal hours x rate calculation.

## Recommendation
For this system, a reasonable quote is:
- 750k-900k NTD for a working event platform with support through launch.
- 900k-1.2m NTD if active feature changes, live incident response, admin training, and post-event cleanup are included.
- Below 600k NTD only if scope is frozen and support is minimal.

Position the offer as: event recruitment platform build, deployment, launch support, admin/recruiter/student workflows, and booking/email reliability.
