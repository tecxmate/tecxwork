## Overview
The flagship career fair event for Vietnamese students in Taiwan, hosted at MCUT (Ming Chi University of Technology) on June 6, 2026.

- **Date**: Saturday, June 6, 2026.
- **Time**: 10:00 AM – 17:30 PM (Taipei Time).

## Configuration
