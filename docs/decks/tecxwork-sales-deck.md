## Slide 2 — The way career fairs run today

Running a career fair today means juggling:

- 📄 Paper sign-up sheets and Google Forms at each booth
- 📊 A spreadsheet per recruiter, reconciled by hand
- 📧 Email chains to schedule and reschedule interviews
- 📎 CVs forwarded around as attachments — no control over who sees what
- 🌐 Students and recruiters who don't share a common language

🗣️ Let them nod. This is their reality. Don't sell yet — just describe their day.

---

## Slide 3 — What that costs you

- **Double-bookings & chaos** at 9 AM when everyone rushes the popular booths
- **No-shows** because there's no confirmation or reminder system
- **Privacy exposure** — CVs with personal data flying around inboxes (a PIPA risk in Taiwan)
- **Staff burnout** — your team hand-reconciling slots instead of running the event
- **No data after** — the event ends and all that talent activity evaporates

🗣️ This is the emotional turn. The hidden tax on every event they've ever run.

## Slide 4 — Meet TECXWORK

**One platform. Three portals. Zero spreadsheets.**

TECXWORK turns the offline scramble into a real-time booking system:

- **Students** browse jobs, build a profile, and book interview slots in one click
- **Recruiters** post roles, open availability, and see applicants + CVs instantly
- **You (organizer)** watch the whole event run itself from one dashboard

🗣️ The reveal. Keep it to one breath — the demo and proof do the convincing.

---

## Slide 5 — How it works

**Student → Recruiter → You, all in sync.**

1. **Before the event** — recruiters onboard, post jobs, open 15-min interview slots. Students register, build profiles, browse the recruiter directory.
2. **Booking opens** — students book slots in real time. The system prevents double-booking automatically. Confirmations and reminders go out by email.
3. **Event day** — everyone arrives with a schedule. Recruiters see their queue; students see their itinerary. You see everything.

🗣️ Walk through one student's journey end-to-end. Concrete > abstract.

## Slide 7 — Privacy by design (built for PIPA)

**CVs never live on our servers.**

- Student CVs stay on the **student's own Google Drive**
- Recruiters get access **only when the student grants it** ("Explicit Targeted Sharing")
- You run a compliant event without becoming a custodian of sensitive personal data

🗣️ For Taiwan/EU organizers this is a real liability lifted off their shoulders. Lead with compliance, not tech.
